Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much can I save with solar?
Typical homes save 70–90% on daytime consumption. Payback ranges from 3–5 years depending on usage and state DISCOM tariffs.
Do you manage net‑metering approvals?
Yes. We prepare documentation, submit to the DISCOM, and coordinate the meter replacement on your behalf.
What brands do you use?
We work with Tier‑1 modules (JA, Trina, Adani) and inverters like Growatt, Sungrow, and Microtek, based on budget and availability.
Is there a warranty?
Standard 25‑year performance warranty on panels, 5–10 years on inverters. Our workmanship warranty covers installation quality.
