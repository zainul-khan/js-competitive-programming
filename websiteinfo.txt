🔑 First, Understand the Purpose of Each Page

Think of each page as having a clear goal, and build content that serves only that goal.

1. Home

Goal: Give a quick overview & lead users to explore deeper.

Keep it short.

Suggested Sections:

Hero section (headline + subheadline + CTA button)

Brief intro to your service or site

3 key features or highlights (small cards with icons)

Testimonials or stats (optional)

Call-to-action again at bottom (contact / get started)

Avoid: Long paragraphs or repeating what's on the About/Services pages.

2. About

Goal: Tell who you/your business is, and why someone should trust you.

Keep it personal and story-driven.

Suggested Sections:

Mission or Vision

Quick Story / Background

Team intro (photos + names + 1-line bios) — if relevant

A small timeline (if there's a history)

A "Why Choose Us?" box with 3–4 bullet points

Avoid: Over-explaining or being too formal — be human.

3. Services

Goal: Clearly explain what you offer.

Structure it visually.

Suggested Sections:

List of services with icons/images

Each service = title + 1-line summary + link to more (optional)

Pricing plans (if applicable)

FAQs (collapsible)

Avoid: Writing 500 words per service. Keep it scannable.

4. Contact

Goal: Make it easy to get in touch.

Minimal content.

Suggested Sections:

Contact form (name, email, message)

Contact info (email, phone, address if needed)

Embedded map (optional)

Social media links

Avoid: Writing big paragraphs here — users just want to reach out quickly.

5. Extras (Optional)

If you're still feeling the site is too short or basic, add:

Blog: For SEO and helpful content

Portfolio or Case Studies: Show your work (great for devs)

Testimonials: Social proof builds trust

🧠 Pro Tip: Use a Consistent Layout

Instead of letting every page scroll too much:

Use sections with equal padding (e.g., 80–100px top/bottom)

Use columns and cards to compress data

Collapse long content (like FAQs)

Use modals or "Learn more" links to avoid stuffing every page
